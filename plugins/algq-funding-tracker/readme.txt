Tracks capital sources, deal-level funding requests, commitments, funded amounts, terms, and funding activity for the Algonquian Real Estate Platform.

== Description ==

Algonquian Funding Tracker provides a controlled administrative record of capital relationships and deal-level financing progress. It does not transfer funds or replace legal, accounting, or executed financing records.
