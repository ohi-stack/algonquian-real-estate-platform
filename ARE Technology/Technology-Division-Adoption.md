# Algonquian Real Estate Technology Division Adoption Record

## Division Name

**Algonquian Real Estate Technology Division**  
Also referred to as: **ARE Technology Division** or **ARE Tech**

## Parent Entity

**Algonquian Real Estate LLC**  
Connecticut limited liability company organized on February 11, 2026.

## Effective Date

**June 1, 2026**

## Adoption Statement

Effective June 1, 2026, Algonquian Real Estate LLC adopts the Algonquian Real Estate Technology Division as an internal operating division of the company.

The division is established for the design, development, maintenance, documentation, and commercialization of proprietary software, automation systems, digital products, technical infrastructure, and technology-enabled workflows supporting the operations and growth of Algonquian Real Estate LLC.

This internal division does not constitute a separate legal entity unless and until separately organized under applicable law. All division activities remain under Algonquian Real Estate LLC unless otherwise documented.

## Division Purpose

The ARE Technology Division exists to:

- Develop proprietary real estate technology.
- Improve acquisition efficiency.
- Automate underwriting and transaction workflows.
- Support document generation and compliance workflows.
- Create scalable software assets.
- Generate technology-based revenue streams.
- Maintain development records for potential research and development substantiation.
- Support future investor, lender, grant, and financing opportunities.
