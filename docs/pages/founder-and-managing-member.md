## Founder Profile

### Gregory Jones

**Founder & Managing Member**

Gregory Jones founded Algonquian Real Estate to build a disciplined Connecticut real estate company centered on acquisition, responsible property operations, structured transaction execution, professional relationships, and long-term enterprise development.

His work includes company strategy, property research, acquisition analysis, seller and investor relationship development, operational documentation, technology architecture, workflow development, marketing, capital preparation, and the continued development of AlgonquianRealEstate.com as both a public website and operating platform.

The current objective is practical: build the systems, relationships, financial discipline, and transaction capacity required for Algonquian Real Estate to source defensible opportunities, make informed decisions, close transactions, operate properties responsibly, and build a verifiable track record.

## Leadership Responsibilities

### Company Strategy & Development

Define company priorities, establish operating systems, develop business relationships, coordinate institutional documentation, and keep resources focused on transaction readiness and sustainable growth.

### Deal Sourcing & Acquisition Strategy

Develop acquisition criteria, evaluate potential opportunities, build seller relationships, review transaction structures, coordinate underwriting, and determine which opportunities merit further diligence or an offer.

### Capital & Professional Relationships

Develop relationships with lenders, private capital sources, buyers, equity partners, attorneys, accountants, brokers, contractors, property managers, and other qualified professionals needed to support transactions.

### Technology & Automation

Direct development of technology that reduces repetitive work, improves conversion, protects company records, strengthens underwriting and documentation, advances transactions, lowers operating costs, or creates reusable commercial value.

## Operating Priorities

**Real estate first. Revenue first. Automation first.**

Company development should be evaluated according to whether it helps Algonquian Real Estate:

- acquire property;
- generate revenue;
- create qualified leads;
- access capital;
- close transactions;
- reduce costs;
- strengthen compliance; or
- build reusable operating value.

Strategic sequence:

**Foundation → First Deal → Track Record → Capital → Portfolio → Scale → Commercial Technology**

## Technology-Enabled Leadership

The page should explain that the ARE technology architecture is organized around the same transaction lifecycle used by the operating company:

```text
Lead → Intake → Qualification → Deal
Underwriting → Strategy → Offer
Documents → Funding → Closing
Operations → Reporting
```

Technology is supporting infrastructure, not a substitute for the real-estate operating business.

## Governance & Accountability

The page must include a visible governance disclosure substantially equivalent to:

> Algonquian Real Estate LLC's Connecticut Certificate of Organization identifies Gregory Jones as Organizer and Managing Member and also identifies Richard Ramirez as a Managing Member. This page profiles Gregory Jones's founder and leadership role and should not be interpreted as a statement that he is the company's sole Managing Member.

Founder leadership does not replace formal company governance, documented authority, professional review, or transaction-specific approval requirements.

## Company-Stage Language

Use grounded first-year-company language. Do not imply a portfolio, revenue history, funding commitments, completed acquisitions, institutional scale, or partnerships unless documented.

Recommended statement:

> Algonquian Real Estate is a first-year Connecticut company building transaction capability, operating discipline, professional relationships, documentation, technology infrastructure, and capital readiness before representing itself as a scaled real estate enterprise.
