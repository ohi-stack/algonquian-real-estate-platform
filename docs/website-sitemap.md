## SEO Sitemap Exclusions

Do not include the following in the public XML sitemap:

- Internal dashboards
- Individual deal records
- Underwriting records
- Offer records
- Investor account pages
- Lender account pages
- Stewardship client records
- Private documents
- Signature pages
- Checkout account data
- Automation logs
- Audit logs
- Administrative pages
- Search-result pages
- Filtered archive URLs

The public XML sitemap should emphasize public company, service, educational, investment, technology, and lead-generation pages. Protected platform routes must remain excluded from search indexing and must be access-controlled independently of SEO settings.
