# Algonquian Real Estate — First-Deal Staffing Plan

**Owner:** Algonquian Real Estate LLC  
**Applies to:** First-deal and early operating stage  
**Primary market:** Connecticut  
**Operating principle:** Real estate-first. Revenue-first. Automation-first.

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum human operating bench Algonquian Real Estate LLC (ARE) should establish before scaling payroll.

ARE is a first-year Connecticut real estate company. At this stage, the objective is not to build a large permanent staff. The objective is to assemble the professional capacity required to source, underwrite, finance, document, close, and stabilize a financially defensible first acquisition while protecting cash.

The staffing model therefore favors:

- outside counsel;
- fractional accounting;
- one part-time operating hire;
- project-based property professionals; and
- relationship-based capital specialists.

Full-time payroll should be added only when recurring revenue or transaction volume supports it.

## 2. Founder Authority

Gregory Jones, as Founder and Managing Member, retains final business authority over:

- acquisition approval;
- negotiated transaction terms;
- financing decisions;
- final offer approval;
- contractual commitments;
- capital deployment;
- closing approval; and
- material operating decisions.

No employee, contractor, agent, software process, or automation may independently bind ARE unless authority is expressly documented.

Governance, ownership, management authority, signing authority, and borrowing authority should remain consistent across ARE's governing documents, banking records, lender applications, and transaction records.

## 3. Top Five Immediate Roles

### Priority 1 — Connecticut Real Estate Attorney

**Engagement model:** Outside counsel / matter-based / transaction-based  
**Classification:** Professional advisor  
**Priority:** Immediate

**Primary purpose:** Protect ARE legally and make transactions executable.

Core responsibilities:

- Review and finalize the Operating Agreement and governance records.
- Clarify ownership, management, signing, and borrowing authority.
- Prepare or review company resolutions where required.
- Review purchase and sale agreements.
- Review seller-financing structures and related business terms.
- Review private-lending and joint-venture documentation where applicable.
- Review Subject-To structures before ARE proceeds.
- Coordinate or advise on title, closing, lien, and contract issues.
- Establish transaction-specific legal controls and escalation points.

**Authority boundary:** Counsel advises and prepares/reviews legal instruments. Final business approval remains with authorized ARE leadership. Transaction-specific legal advice must come from qualified Connecticut counsel.

**Success condition:** ARE can enter a transaction with documented authority, reviewed agreements, defined legal responsibilities, and a controlled closing process.

---

### Priority 2 — Bookkeeper / CPA

**Engagement model:** Fractional monthly bookkeeping with CPA review as needed  
**Classification:** Finance and accounting support  
**Priority:** Immediate

**Primary purpose:** Make ARE financially controlled, tax-ready, and lender-reviewable.

Core responsibilities:

- Maintain the accounting system and chart of accounts.
- Track startup expenses and owner contributions.
- Reconcile business bank accounts.
- Produce monthly profit-and-loss and balance-sheet reports.
- Maintain cash-flow reporting and debt schedules.
- Track ARE Technology Division expenses separately where appropriate.
- Establish property-level accounting after acquisition.
- Support tax preparation and lender financial requests.
- Reconcile accounting records to bank statements and funding applications.

**Initial planning range:** approximately $200-$400 per month for basic fractional bookkeeping, subject to actual provider scope and pricing.

**Success condition:** ARE can produce current, internally consistent financial statements and supporting records without reconstructing them at the time of a lender request.

---

### Priority 3 — Acquisition & Lead Manager

**Engagement model:** Part-time employee or contractor, initially approximately 10-20 hours per week  
**Classification:** Primary early operating hire  
**Priority:** Immediate after legal/accounting setup begins

**Primary purpose:** Keep qualified property opportunities entering and advancing through the ARE acquisition pipeline.

Core responsibilities:

- Property-owner outreach.
- Tired-landlord and absentee-owner prospecting.
- Vacant and distressed-property lead development.
- Inherited-property and seller-financing opportunity follow-up where appropriate.
- Follow-up calls, approved email/text follow-up, and appointment scheduling.
- Initial property-information gathering.
- CRM data quality and next-action maintenance.
- Lead qualification and referral-source follow-up.
- Broker, wholesaler, attorney, vendor, and referral relationship follow-up within approved boundaries.

**System boundary:**

Deal Intake remains the authoritative entry point for new acquisition opportunities. Pipeline CRM owns the canonical deal after acceptance. The Acquisition & Lead Manager operates those systems but does not replace their data authority.

**Human authority boundary:** The Acquisition & Lead Manager may gather information, qualify, schedule, follow up, and prepare records. Final pricing, offer terms, contracts, financing commitments, and closing decisions require authorized ARE approval.

**Core KPIs:**

- new owner contacts;
- qualified leads;
- follow-ups completed;
- appointments scheduled;
- properties submitted for underwriting;
- offers generated;
- contracts produced; and
- lead-to-next-stage conversion rate.

**Initial planning range:** approximately $600-$1,000 per month for limited part-time support, subject to classification, hours, compensation law, and actual engagement terms.

**Success condition:** Every qualified lead has an assigned owner, next action, due date where applicable, and current CRM status.

---

### Priority 4 — Contractor / Rehab Estimator

**Engagement model:** Preferred vendor / project-based  
**Classification:** Property diligence support  
**Priority:** Before committing to material value-add rehabilitation

**Primary purpose:** Prevent inaccurate repair assumptions from undermining underwriting.

Core responsibilities:

- Evaluate visible property condition.
- Develop preliminary scopes of work and repair budgets.
- Identify areas requiring licensed specialists.
- Estimate labor and material ranges.
- Identify contingency needs.
- Support contractor bid comparison.
- Revisit assumptions as diligence becomes more complete.

Typical review categories:

- roofing;
- foundation;
- electrical;
- plumbing;
- HVAC;
- windows;
- kitchens and bathrooms;
- flooring;
- interior and exterior work;
- code-related repair items;
- unit turns; and
- deferred maintenance.

**Authority boundary:** A contractor/estimator does not replace licensed inspectors, engineers, electricians, plumbers, environmental professionals, code officials, or other specialists where their expertise is required.

**Success condition:** MAO and acquisition decisions use a written repair scope and budget that is traceable to property-specific diligence rather than informal assumptions.

---

### Priority 5 — Capital & Lending Specialist

**Engagement model:** Relationship-based / deal-based / appropriately licensed professional where required  
**Classification:** Financing support  
**Priority:** Build relationship before ARE has a deal under contract

**Primary purpose:** Determine how a financially defensible acquisition can realistically be funded.

Core responsibilities:

- Match transactions to appropriate lenders or capital structures.
- Explain down-payment, reserve, DSCR, guaranty, and documentation requirements.
- Evaluate bridge, permanent, DSCR, private, seller-financing, and other appropriate pathways.
- Identify financing gaps early.
- Maintain a lender/capital-source matrix.
- Help ARE understand lender packaging requirements before submission.
- Support refinance and exit-financing planning.

**Authority boundary:** The Capital & Lending Specialist may identify and coordinate financing pathways but may not make unapproved commitments on behalf of ARE. Securities, brokerage, lending, referral, and compensation activities must remain within applicable law and licensing requirements.

**Success condition:** Before ARE signs a transaction that depends on financing, leadership has a documented capital path, required-cash estimate, underwriting constraints, and contingency plan.

## 4. Hiring / Engagement Sequence

Use this sequence unless a live transaction creates a documented reason to accelerate a later role:

1. Establish Connecticut real estate counsel relationship.
2. Establish fractional bookkeeping/accounting support.
3. Engage a part-time Acquisition & Lead Manager.
4. Build a preferred contractor/rehab-estimator bench.
5. Build a capital/lending relationship bench.

## 5. Initial Cost-Control Standard

ARE currently intends to preserve cash for acquisition-related activity and use outside employment income to support the company, with a planning ceiling of up to $2,000 per month subject to personal obligations and actual availability.

The initial staffing target should therefore keep recurring fixed personnel overhead approximately within:

| Function | Initial planning approach |
| --- | ---: |
| Fractional bookkeeping | $200-$400/month |
| Part-time acquisition/lead support | $600-$1,000/month |
| Attorney | Reserve / matter-based |
| Contractor / estimator | Per property |
| Capital specialist | Relationship / deal-based |
| **Target recurring fixed personnel overhead** | **Approximately $800-$1,400/month** |

These figures are planning assumptions, not executed contracts, guaranteed pricing, wage determinations, or commitments.

Remaining available monthly business cash should preferentially support:

- lead generation;
- inspections and diligence;
- legal review;
- accounting;
- property research;
- required deposits or transaction costs; and
- operating reserves.

## 7. Platform and Agent Controls

ARE's software and agents support the human operating bench but do not replace professional or executive authority.

- Deal Intake owns submission-time seller/property records.
- Pipeline CRM owns the canonical Deal record.
- MAO Engine owns underwriting calculations and versioned analysis.
- Offer Generator owns approved proposal/offer generation.
- Document Library owns controlled document records.
- PDF & Signature Engine supports approved document rendering/signature workflows.
- Funding Tracker owns funding-source and financing-status records.
- Automation Engine coordinates approved workflow events.
- Admin Command Center provides oversight and reporting.

Agents may research, organize, draft, calculate through authorized systems, recommend, route, monitor, and prepare actions. Human approval remains required for final negotiated terms, contractual commitments, funds movement, legal conclusions, financing commitments, and closing authority unless separately documented.

## 8. Roles ARE Should Not Prioritize Yet

### Full-Time Software Developer

ARE already has substantial technology infrastructure and a dedicated Technology Division. Additional software development should be funded when it materially removes labor, improves conversion, protects records, enables a transaction, or produces measurable revenue.

A full-time software developer is not a first-deal staffing priority.

### Large Permanent Administrative Staff

Do not add permanent overhead merely to appear institutional. Early staffing must be justified by lead generation, transaction execution, compliance, financial control, property diligence, capital access, or recurring revenue.

## 9. Next Hiring Tier After First Transactions

Once transaction volume or recurring revenue supports additional payroll, evaluate:

1. Transaction Coordinator.
2. Property / Asset Manager.
3. Operations Manager.
4. Dedicated Underwriting / Acquisitions Analyst.
5. Expanded Acquisition Manager or team.

The trigger for these roles is documented workload and revenue, not organizational appearance.

## 10. Governing Rule

> Hire or engage a person only when the role materially helps ARE generate qualified leads, improve underwriting, access capital, reduce transaction risk, close properties, operate assets, protect records, or create repeatable revenue.

Technology and staffing should both be judged against the same question: **Does this materially advance a real estate transaction, revenue stream, capital relationship, operating control, or defensible company capability?**
